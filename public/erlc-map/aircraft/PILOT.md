# Pilot Base

Ready Player Me rigged sample avatar, copyright Ready Player Me.
Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/readyplayer.me.glb
Avatar licensing reference: https://readyplayerme.hashnode.dev/how-to-create-3d-characters-for-your-unity-game-with-our-avatar-sdk

The avatar retains its original copyright and is not covered by the application's
MIT license. The runtime adds a seated pose, articulated control-following hands,
limb inverse kinematics and an aviation headset. The model is bundled locally;
no avatar service is contacted by the simulator.
